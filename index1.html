Admission Checklist

1. Follow the current Maharashtra FYJC Centralised Admission Process (CAP) instructions where applicable.

2. Complete the required online registration and preferences.

3. Review merit/allotment updates and the college's instructions.

4. Submit required documents and complete verification within the stated deadline.

Commonly requested documents

• SSC marksheet and passing certificate

• School leaving / transfer certificate

• Recent photograph and identity details

• Caste / category certificate, if applicable

• Any additional documents listed in the official admission notice

Please verify: Subject combinations, optional subjects, eligibility, seat availability, dates and the admission process can change. This student-project page is a general guide, not an official admission notice.

Promotion & Admission Steps

• Check the college's official notice and academic calendar.

• Confirm promotion requirements and subject combination.

• Complete the college's SYJC continuation / admission formalities.

• Pay applicable fees only through the official process.

Keep ready

• FYJC marksheet / result

• College identity or enrolment details

• Required continuation forms

• Any documents requested by the college office

The exact subject combination depends on the Maharashtra State Board scheme and the subjects offered/selected by the student. Confirm the current list with SCCT before relying on it.
